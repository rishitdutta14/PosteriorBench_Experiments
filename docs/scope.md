# Scope and interpretation

The common question is how posterior evaluation relates to uncertainty structure and physical consistency. The five studies provide complementary evidence with different controls.

| Study | What it can establish | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial metric sensitivity | Detectability of specified dependence changes under calibrated Gaussian controls | Does not represent every spatial discrepancy or evaluate a particular solver |
| Sensor placement | Information remaining under a fixed Gaussian Poisson prior; a final 4×/512-member ES-MDA comparison | Not a universal optimal sensor design; injected and assumed noise differ |
| Poisson geometry | Spread and linear covariance geometry for the recorded released-profile rerun | One verified training shard; nonlinear dimension unresolved |
| Darcy | Pairwise pressure inconsistency and consequences of re-solving the same saved permeability | Not a general claim that enforcing physics harms inference |
| LTMI | Distributional light-detail improvement and errors hidden by layer averaging | Internal light maps are not independently rendered responses |

The weighted reference ensembles are finite estimates. Case counts refer to benchmark cases, not to independent pixels. Pixelwise quantities are aggregated within cases before reporting case averages, except where a pooled calculation is explicitly labelled.

The official evaluator outputs describe these particular reruns. The original paper's per-case random draws were unavailable. Matching a published algorithm/configuration should not be confused with reproducing every stochastic outcome in the original paper.
